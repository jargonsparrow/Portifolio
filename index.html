How I Test

A repeatable approach to web application assessments

  1. 01

    Recon

    Map the app: pages, parameters, technologies and entry points.

  2. 02

    Test

    Probe for OWASP Top 10 issues by hand and with Burp Suite.

  3. 03

    Validate

    Confirm each finding is real and safe to reproduce.

  4. 04

    Report

    Rate the risk and write clear reproduction steps.

  5. 05

    Fix

    Recommend practical fixes and re-test them.

Ethics: I only test my own lab environments, intentionally vulnerable apps, and systems I have written permission to assess.
